# Privacy Policy for GATE Guard

**Last Updated:** September 21, 2026

## 1. Data Collection
GATE Guard is designed with strict privacy in mind. **We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data.** 
- No user data is sent to external servers.
- The extension operates entirely locally on your device.

## 2. Permissions Used
The extension requests the following permissions for functional purposes only:
- **`*://*.youtube.com/*`**: Used strictly to monitor YouTube pages to block distracting content.
- **`storage`**: Used only to save your local extension settings on your device.
- **`tabs` & `webNavigation`**: Used to detect when you navigate to a new YouTube video so the blocking script can run immediately without page reloads.

## 3. Third-Party Services
GATE Guard does not use any third-party analytics, tracking scripts, or advertising networks.
